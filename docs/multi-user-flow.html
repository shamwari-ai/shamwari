What the board is actually showing

Every lane runs the same six stages, because there is one code path. What differs is where a lane stops and why. Three divergences are worth watching for.

Chipo's lane never reaches a provider

She asks about her own payslip, so her request is personal scope. While MIND_AVAILABLE=false it is refused at the scope gate with 409 scope_requires_local_inference — two stages before anything is embedded and four before anything is generated.

Flip the switch and her lane changes shape rather than lengthening: it picks up Ground and then stops. /v1/ground/context returns the system prompt and the citations, and her device generates the answer. The gateway never sees it, which is why there is no Sink pip and no row in the log. That is what "routes to Mind" has to mean when Mind runs on the device — Mind is a client of the gateway, not a backend behind it.

Farai escalates and the others do not

His question about VAT deferment is long and contains a hard keyword, so routing-policy.json sends him to the standard tier and Kimi K3. Tendai and Nomsa ask short questions and stay on economy, which is where the margin comes from — AI Gateway passes provider pricing through at cost.

Break the credential and nothing fails

The Infer stage has three steps behind it: AI Gateway, then the provider directly, then Workers AI as the last resort. Every response carries inference_path so the degradation is observable rather than assumed. Break both switches at once and traffic lands on @cf/qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8 — slower, cheaper, still answering.

These paths rot silently if they are never exercised, because on a healthy day nothing reaches them: step 2 runs only when step 1 fails, step 3 only when both do. A weekly cron now exercises all three independently and writes the reading to platform.serviceHealth, so a rotted fallback is a row you can query rather than a monthly chore someone has to remember — and skipping a chore looks exactly like passing it.

Two gates on the training corpus, not one

A conversation reaches mind_training_chunks only if both hold:

  • The teacher model permits it. Kimi K3 and Qwen do; Claude and GPT do not. Resolved from the model that actually answered, against an allowlist in code where a policy file cannot reach it — not from the tier that was requested. Flip the Dynamic route switch: the tier column still says standard, the served model becomes Claude, and the row stops being training material. Nothing was deployed to make that happen, which is the point.
  • It was not the user's own data. A personal-scope exchange is never training material however permissive the teacher model's licence was. That is the user's gate, and it is separate.

Both are enforced at write time. Postgres carries CHECK (license_class = 'open_weight') underneath, so a restricted row physically cannot enter the table.

The honest gap

Ground misses on most lanes here because it would. Twenty-two corpus sources are seeded and five are blocked pending a licence read; the Constitution of Zimbabwe is the only source currently approved, ground-eligible and mind-eligible. Ingest it and the Ground column starts telling the truth.
