> A friend that serves; a friend that does not control.

Shamwari means *friend* in Shona. It is the community pillar of the Bundu
Ecosystem, and it is a companion rather than a chatbot: it is meant to know
things about your life, which is exactly why the rules about where your data
can go are the most important thing on this site.

## Start here

Two constraints shape every other decision, and they are the two worth
reading before anything else:

- **[The two rules](/rules/)** — personal data never reaches a third-party
  model, and only open-weight output may train Shamwari Mind. Both are
  enforced in code in more than one place.
- **[Data scopes](/scopes/)** — the distinction that matters in Shamwari is
  not how much you pay. It is whose data a question is about.

Then, if you want the mechanics:

- **[Architecture](/architecture/)** — two services, drawn along one line.
- **[Routing and models](/routing/)** — which model answers, and why the
  provenance of the answer is recorded at the moment it is generated.
- **[Ground and citation](/ground/)** — how an answer about Zimbabwean law
  gets a section number attached to it.
- **[How we describe it](/language/)** — the words we use, and the ones we
  refuse to, because the difference is load-bearing.

## What Shamwari is not

It is not a model we trained. Shamwari Cloud routes questions to models
published by other people; Shamwari Mind, which runs on your device, is the
one we train. Keeping those two straight is not modesty, it is the only
version of the story that survives someone checking.
